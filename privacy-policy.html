Stashrush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Stashrush is a game made by Fjordlight Studio ("we", "us"). Contact: rkjersem@gmail.com

1. What stays on your device

The game has no accounts and no login. Your progress (cash, boosters, trophies, league, settings and simple game counters) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's storage in your Android settings.

2. Online duels

When you start an online duel, the game sends the nickname you chose (up to 12 characters), your selected character and booster, and your trophy count to our matchmaking server, and shares them with your opponent. During the duel your moves are relayed between the two devices. The server keeps this only in memory while you are matched or playing; we do not store it in a database or link it to who you are. The server is hosted by Render in Frankfurt, Germany. As with most web hosting, the host may process your IP address in technical logs for security and operation. If no opponent is found, you play against a computer opponent. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as a nickname.

3. Advertising

Stashrush shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. When you choose to watch one, Google and its ad partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about how you interact with ads, to show ads and measure them. In the EEA, UK and other regions where required, we ask for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice later under "Ad privacy choices" in the game's settings. You can also reset your advertising ID in your Android settings. Read more: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. Google tells us that a purchase was made so we can give you the items.

5. Children

Stashrush is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws you may ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to processing. We keep no account or profile about you. Nicknames and match data exist only in server memory during a duel. For data held by Google (ads and purchases), use the privacy tools in your Google account. You can also contact us at the address above, for example to request deletion.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. The recipients are your opponent in an online duel (nickname, character, booster and trophy count), Google (AdMob and Google Play) when you use ads or purchases, and our hosting provider Render, which runs the matchmaking server. Connections to the server are encrypted.

8. International transfers

Google and our hosting provider may process data outside your country. They use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
